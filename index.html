01 / ABOUT ME

What I'm interested in
and why.

I'm a high school student in France, and I haven't decided on one exact career yet. I want to keep exploring science and engineering before I choose a direction.

Biology interests me because I'm curious about how different substances affect the body how some can help or heal, while others can be harmful. I'm also interested in plants and their properties.

Space makes me wonder how people build machines that travel beyond Earth, and how scientists learn about objects that are incredibly far away. Physics is another way for me to ask questions about how the world works.

I enjoy programming because I can make something from an idea. Robotics appeals to me for a similar reason: I like the thought of building something and seeing it work in real life.
